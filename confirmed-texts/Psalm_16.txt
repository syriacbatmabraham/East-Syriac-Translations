Keep me, O God, for I have trusted in You

(Qanona) Glorious is Your trust, O Fashioner; in it my weakness rejoices
Keep me, O God, for I have trusted in You
I have said to Mar Yah: You are my Lord
And my good - it is from beside You
And as for the Holy Ones on earth and the glorious ones
All my delight is in them
Their last pains increase quickly
I do not pour their libation from blood
And I do not commemorate their names with my lips
Mar Yah, the portion of my inheritance and my cup
You are restoring to me my inheritance
Lines have fallen to me in choice [places]
And even my inheritance has been pleasing to me
I bless Mar Yah, who has counseled me
And even in the nights my depths are instructing me
I have set Mar Yah before me at all times
And He has been at my right hand, that I may not be shaken
Because of this, my heart has rejoiced and my honor has exulted
And even my flesh lives in serenity
For You have not forsaken my soul in Sheol
And You have not given Your Devout One over to see corruption
You show me Your Way of life
And I am filled from the joy of Your Face
And from the sweetness of the victory of Your Right Hand
