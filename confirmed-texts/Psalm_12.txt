Save, Mar Yah, for good has come to an end

(Qanona) Deceit has increased, and love itself has abated; M'shiha, do not delay
Save, Mar Yah, for good has come to an end
And faithfulness has ceased from the earth
The sons of men speak emptiness
A man with his fellow in divided lips
In doubled hearts they speak
Mar Yah destroys all divided lips
And tongues that speak great things
Who have said: We will magnify our tongue
And our lips are our own; who is our Lord?
Because of the plundering of the poor and the groaning of the miserable
Mar Yah has said: I will now arise
And I openly bring about salvation
The utterance of Mar Yah is a pure utterance
Choice silver tried in the earth
And purified seven times
And You, Mar Yah, keep them
Rescue me and deliver me unto Eternity from this generation
wherein the wicked circle about and walk
as [at] the contemptible high place of the sons of Edom
