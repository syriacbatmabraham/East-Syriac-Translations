He who is dwelling in the shelter of the Most High
And is being glorified in the shadow of God
(Qanona) You are my trust, M'shiha; unto Eternity I will not be ashamed
He who is dwelling in the shelter of the Most High
And is being glorified in the shadow of God
Say to Mar Yah: My trust
The House of my refuge, the God in whom I trust
For He delivers you from the snare of stumbling
And from speech of emptiness
With His pinions He delivers you, and under His wings you are sheltered
[As] armor, His Truth surrounds you
You fear not from the fear of night
Nor from the arrow flying by daylight
Nor from the word walking in darkness
Nor from the ruha of a demon at noon
From your side, thousands fall
And myriads from your right hand
And to you they do not draw near
Rather, with your eyes alone, you see
You see the recompense of the wicked
For You, Mar Yah, are my trust
Who in the Highest have set Your dwelling
Evil does not draw near to you
And affliction does not draw near your tent
For He commands His angels concerning you
To guard you in all your ways
And upon their arms they bear you
So that you do not stumble with your foot
Upon the viper and the serpent you tread
And you trample the lion and the dragon
Because he has sought Me, I deliver him and strengthen him
Because he has known My Name, he calls Me and I answer him
I am with him in distress
I strengthen him and honor him
With length of days I satisfy him, and I show him My salvation
