Mar Yah, who will dwell in Your Tabernacle?

(Qanona) In purity of thoughts, our Lord, raise me before Your Madbaha
Mar Yah, who will dwell in Your Tabernacle?
And who will live on Your holy Mountain?
The one walking without blemish and doing righteousness
Speaking truth in his heart, and not deceitful with his tongue
And not doing evil to his fellow
And not accepting a bribe against his neighbor
Despised in his eyes is the provoker
And he honors those who fear Mar Yah
Swearing to his fellow and not lying
And not lending his money at interest
And not taking a bribe against the innocent
Whoever is doing these things - he is just
And unshaken for Eternity
