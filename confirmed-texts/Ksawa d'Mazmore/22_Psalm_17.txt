Hear, O Holy Mar Yah, and look upon my pleading
And listen to my prayer, which is not from deceitful lips
(Qanona) My Lord and my God, have mercy on me, for I am being pursued unrightly
Hear, O Holy Mar Yah, and look upon my pleading
And listen to my prayer, which is not from deceitful lips
From before You my justice goes forth
And Your eyes see uprightness
You have examined my heart, and You have visited me in the night
And You have tested me, and You have not found unrighteousness in me
And the works of the sons of men have not passed over my mouth
In speech of the lips
Rather, You have kept me from evil ways
You have supported my paces on Your paths, so that my steps are unshaken
I have called You, for You have answered me
O God, incline Your ear toward me, and hear my words
Make Your Devout One a wonder
And a Savior to those who hope
From those who stand against Your Right Hand
Guard me like the pupil of an eye
And hide me in the shadow of Your wings
From before these sinners who have plundered me
And the enemies of my soul who have set [themselves] against me
Shut the mouth of those who have spoken arrogance
They have glorified me, and now they have encircled me
They have set their eyes to cast me down to the earth
They resemble a lion seeking to crush
Like a lion's cub sitting in hiding
Arise, Mar Yah, before their faces, and make them kneel
And deliver my soul from the wicked and from the Sword
And from the dead who are dying from Your hands, Mar Yah
And from the dead of the grave
You separate them in life
Your hoards have filled their belly
They are satisfied with sons, and leave what remains to their sons
But I - in righteousness - I see Your Face
And I am satisfied when Your faithfulness has awakened
