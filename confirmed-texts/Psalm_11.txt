In Mar Yah I have hoped, how are you saying to my soul:
Wander and dwell upon the mountains like a bird
(Qanona) Sinners have schemed against me; in You, Mar Yah, I have hoped
In Mar Yah I have hoped, how are you saying to my soul:
Wander and dwell upon the mountains like a bird
For, behold, sinners have drawn the bow
And have set their arrows upon the string
To shoot in obscurity at the upright of heart
For what You have prepared, they have thrown down
But the Righteous One, what is He doing?
Mar Yah, in the Haykla of His holiness
Mar Yah, in Heaven, His Seat
His eyes are seeing and His eyelids are examining the sons of men
Mar Yah is examining the righteous and the unrighteous
And the lovers of unrighteousness, His soul has hated
Snares have come down upon the wicked like rain
Fire and brimstone
And a ruha of affliction, the portion of their cup
For Mar Yah is righteous, and He loves righteousness
And His Face is seeing uprightness
