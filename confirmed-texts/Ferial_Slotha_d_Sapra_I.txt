O Gracious One, O Merciful One, O Tender One
Whose door is open to those who return
And who continually calls sinners, that unto Him they may draw near for returning
Open, our Lord and our God, the door of mercies to our prayer
And accept our pleading
And render, in Your mercies, our requests
From the house of Your treasure, rich and overflowing
O Good One, who does not withhold His mercies and His gifts from the needy and afflicted, His servants who call and beseech Him
In all times and seasons
The Lord of all, the Father, the Son, and the Ruha d'Qudsha, forever
