How long, Mar Yah? Will You forget me for Eternity?
How long will You turn Your Face from me?
(Qanona) Be reconciled to me and save me; that I may confess unto You, Mar Yah
How long, Mar Yah? Will You forget me for Eternity?
How long will You turn Your Face from me?
How long will You place sorrow in my soul?
And misery in my heart every day?
How long will my enemy be exalted over me?
Look and answer me, Mar Yah my God
And enlighten my eyes, that I may not sleep unto death
And that my enemy may not say: I have conquered him
Nor my oppressors rejoice over me when I tremble
Nevertheless, I have trusted in Your grace
My heart rejoices in Your salvation
And I glorify Mar Yah, for He has saved me
